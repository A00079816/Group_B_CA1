Assignment Overview
You will research and analyse a significant development in computing history
through two distinct viewpoints, demonstrating your understanding of how
computing innovations impact different stakeholders. This assignment requires
you to think critically about technology's role in society and business while
considering ethical implications and proposing improvements.
Assignment Task
Select ONE significant development in computing history and produce a
comprehensive case study that:
1. Tracks the historical evolution of your chosen development from origin to present day
2. Analyses the development through TWO viewpoints from the following:
o Scientific
o Engineering
o Business
o Societal
o Mathematical
3. Evaluates ethical implications and proposes realistic improvements
